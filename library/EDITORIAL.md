# Library editorial standard

A prompt should have a distinct job, be easy to understand and deliver a useful result. The copied text includes the authority, evidence, scope, stop condition and output that the person needs. Recipes compose existing capabilities without contradictory instructions; do not add duplicates for the sake of count.

`library.json` is the source for entries, categories, replacement relationships and recipes. Preserve stable IDs and source credit. Record real trials accurately; do not call a reader's impression a test.

`audit.json` contains earlier editorial observations, overlap groups and historical scores consumed by the exported inventory. Retain useful evidence and references; new entries do not need a score or an audit row. The validator checks existing records for referential integrity, not whether an entry has earned an arbitrary number. Rebuild exports and affected shipped references when the source changes.
