# init

Writes PRODUCT.md: who the product is for and what it should feel like. Every other job reads it, so a critique judges against this product and not against design in general.

Interactive. Writes one file, after a yes.

## If PRODUCT.md already exists

Read it. Do not overwrite it, whatever tool wrote it. Say in three lines what it covers and what is missing from the list below, and ask whether to fill the gaps. Add only what they approve, keeping their wording and structure.

## Steps

1. **Learn what you can without asking.** Read the README, the home or landing screen, the sign-up flow, the navigation labels and any marketing copy. Open the running product if you can. Draft an answer to every item below from what you find.
2. **Ask only what the project could not tell you.** Put the questions in one message, six at most, each with your drafted answer so a "yes" is enough. Typical gaps: who exactly uses it, what they are in the middle of when they open it, and what it must never feel like.
3. **Show the draft file and ask for a yes.** Then write it to the project root.

## What goes in the file

Keep it under one page. Short sentences. No adjectives that could describe any product ("clean", "modern", "intuitive").

- **What it is**, in one sentence a stranger would understand.
- **Who uses it.** One to three kinds of people, each with what they are trying to get done and how much patience they have. A real example beats a persona.
- **The moment of use.** Where they are and what they are doing when they open it: at a desk for an hour, or on a phone for ten seconds between other things.
- **The one thing each main screen is for.** A line per screen. If a screen has two jobs, write both and note it.
- **How it should feel**, as three plain contrasts: "calm, not sparse", "dense, not cramped". The second half of each is the mistake to avoid.
- **What must never happen.** The failures that would cost trust: a wrong number shown as right, a lost draft, a surprise charge.
- **Products it should sit beside.** Two or three the users already know, and what to take from each.

## Report

Follow the report shape in SKILL.md. "What you need to do" is usually to read the file once and correct anything that sounds wrong. If there is no DESIGN.md, say so under "Not checked" and that Keen cannot write one yet.
